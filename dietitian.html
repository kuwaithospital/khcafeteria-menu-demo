    Tray Runner

    تطبيق توصيل الوجبات

    A handheld app for porters and food-service assistants. Trays move from the kitchen to the bedside with patient identity checks at every hand-over.

    1. Ready for pickupKitchen marks a tray ready. It appears here instantly, sorted by delivery time.
    2. Pick up trayRunner collects it. The TV dashboard and patient screen switch to “On the way”.
    3. Verify at the bedsideScan the wristband and confirm two patient identifiers and the diet before handing over.
    4. DeliveredTime-stamped on-time / late record feeds the kitchen and TV statistics.
